Fix it

Finds anyone with exactly two identified parents who aren't yet tagged as spouses of each other, and marries them automatically — that's always a safe, sure thing. It can't guess an actual relation label for anyone (Father vs Sister vs Aunt), so those get listed for you to fill in by hand.

For sharing / diagnosing

If you want help troubleshooting without sharing real names, dates, or photos — these swap everyone's name for "Person 1", "Person 2" etc. (Me stays "Me"), and strip photos, dates, places and descriptions. The relationships and layout stay intact, which is all that's usually needed to spot what's going wrong.
